# TAP-VWAP-01: Proof of Exchange (research draft)

## Scope and admission
Bitcoin-profile BTC/Taproot Asset spot conversion index for TradeLayer and SatsHedgeZero. Isolated from the zero-dependency consensus crate; no change to Cargo, ledger, oracle publication, rewards or consensus. Current reference model is synthetic arithmetic, NOT a settlement verifier, ZK prover, live feed or permission to trade. Test asset only until an independently authenticated issuance identity and decimal policy are pinned. USDT units price BTC in USDT, not necessarily USD during a depeg.

Existing integration surfaces: [volume index](../../docs/volume-index.md), [oracle metadata](../../docs/ORACLE.md), [supply chain](../../security/RUST_SUPPLY_CHAIN.md). Source references: [tapd ForwardingHistory](https://lightning.engineering/api-docs/api/taproot-assets/rfq/forwarding-history/), [RFQ guide](https://github.com/lightninglabs/taproot-assets/blob/main/docs/rfq.md). Pin exact tapd and lnd revisions before native experiments. RFQ accepted-message signatures prove quote terms; RPC history is a node's assertion. Neither alone proves an exchange.

## Receipt boundary
A conversion is one economic exchange at an edge, not each hop of its Lightning route. A payment with two real asset/BTC conversions has two legs. MPP/partial fills count each independently settled conversion exactly once, subject to the RFQ aggregate limit. The canonical identity is the asset-side funding outpoint, offerer side relative to agreed channel ordering, HTLC ID and chain genesis. Resolve aliases and splices to authenticated channel state; two observers of one conversion derive the same identity. No reporter or epoch salt. Nullifier = domain-separated collision-resistant hash of canonical identity; private channel identifiers require a separate privacy analysis (public nullifiers can be linkable). Reject unsupported splices and ambiguous legs. Maintain spent-nullifier root across windows.

Normalize base quantity B in millisatoshis of the BTC leg at the edge, U in asset atoms delivered in the opposite direction, with fees separately accounted for. For each RFQ policy type derive the appropriate BTC-side amount from actual ingress/egress evidence; never sum in+out, double-count a route, or use the quoted maximum. Define a principal-only index; reject conversions whose fee attribution is unknown, and record a separate all-in price if desired. Asset-to-asset forwards, refunds, cancellations, failed/expired HTLCs excluded. Use actual settled partial amounts; rate rounding must follow the pinned tapd implementation. Signed quote must bind session, side, asset, accepted quantities, rate and expiry; check actual wire semantics, including whether a signed request/response transcript is necessary to bind these fields. For quote reuse maintain a cumulative consumed quantity state keyed by canonical signer+RFQ ID, across epochs.

Proposed private witness: request/accept transcript, signed channel states before and after, authenticated funding ancestry and asset commitment/transition evidence, matched HTLC/custom records, fulfillment hash/preimage, fee decomposition and operator membership. Verify both legs and balance conservation. Prove that the relevant settled state is authenticated, with a precise revocation/latest-state and finality model. A preimage does not establish balance movement. Standard Lightning may not expose enough mutually authenticated state to prove this relation; derive it from pinned implementations in TAP-VWAP-02. Alternative first tier: both economic counterparties sign a domain-separated settlement receipt, validated by ZK; this proves agreement, not honest channel settlement against collusion. A one-sided tapd signature is only an attestation. Do not label either as trustless settlement.

## Public statement and recurrence
Policy hash commits chain, index ID, asset identity, decimals, fee definition, operator registry snapshot, proof/verifier version, epoch rules, price rounding and risk thresholds. Public inputs: policy hash, epoch ID, previous accepted global nullifier and quote-consumption roots, authenticated batch commitment, prior publication digest, opening/closing anchor references. Public outputs: next roots, aggregate base/quote amounts, count, eligible group coverage, exact price in q8 units, validity status and commitment to robust group statistics. Public totals may leak small-window trades; apply minimum activity and privacy review before publishing.

For each accepted receipt verify the witness relation, policy membership, eligibility/time bounds, range constraints, nonmembership of nullifier and update, quote cumulative maximum, and append commitments. State recurrence (S_B,S_U,n) -> (S_B+B,S_U+U,n+1). Use bounded integer limbs and checked additions; constrain every intermediate to prevent field wrap. For asset decimals d=6, q8 USDT/BTC = floor_half_up(10^13 S_U / S_B); general scale is 10^(19-d), or rational scaling for d>19. With positive denominator, prove N=qD+r, 0<=r<D and rounded=q+[2r>=D]. Reject empty, zero, overflow or stale windows. Aggregate raw quantities before division; never average rounded per-trade rates. Cross-check q8 price direction with TradeLayer property IDs. Keep msat precision until final reporting; do not silently truncate to the existing eight-decimal volume API.

Nova folds receipt steps with a persistent authenticated nullifier/quote state. A final wrapper may verify an epoch on NEAR or through an audited Bitcoin-compatible verifier. Benchmark secp256k1/SHA256/Taproot evidence separately from cheap arithmetic before choosing curves or compression. No requirement for STARKs. No production verifier accepted by this draft.

## Time, completeness, and finality
RPC timestamps are hints. A peer-signed timestamp proves what was claimed. For first admitted feed use a registered batch log with sequence and prior-root commitments, a bounded Bitcoin-anchor-height admission window, and publication deadlines; it proves receipt inclusion by a deadline, not exact wall-clock execution time. Prove coverage of committed log prefixes and detect sequence gaps; omission before log admission remains possible. Maintain explicit watermarks, missing-feed flags, data availability/challenge procedures, and fork/rollback rules for each anchor. Do not conflate NEAR finality, Bitcoin finality and local clock time. High-frequency indicative quotes and slower finalized settlement windows are distinct. Consumers halt or use an explicitly governed fallback on missing/stale/unsafe epochs. No implicit last-price carry for liquidation.

## Robust index and economic model
Publish raw volume-weighted price as a diagnostic. Compare median of per-control-group VWAPs (equal weight) and capped-weight variants. Registered operator keys map to capital-backed control groups fixed before epoch opening; common ownership and Sybil resistance are external assumptions. A cap on channel balance alone cannot bound repeated round trips. Evaluate turnover vs paid non-refundable fee, net inventory, independent price sources and derivative exposure. Equal-group median with strictly more than half honest eligible groups whose prices lie in [L,H] stays in [L,H], conditional on honest group availability and grouping correctness. Thresholds for minimum volume, group count, maximum group concentration and disagreement determine whether to publish; freeze parameters before calibration. Quote prices sourced from one external venue imply shared price dependency. A valid proof never establishes organic demand, representative sampling or manipulation-free market prices.

If organic base volume V, attacker volume M, relative price offset delta, raw distortion = delta*M/(V+M). To move raw index by e with 0<e<delta, M>=V*e/(delta-e). A fee floor f on executed notional gives a conditional marginal expense, not a security proof; rebates, collusion, capital recycling and cross-market profit matter. Cap open interest using stressed profit bounds versus defensible attack cost and explicitly account for liquidations.

## Registered stages
- TAP-VWAP-01A: synthetic normalized receipts, exact integer aggregate, atomic rejection, duplicate/epoch checks, equal-group median. Include single malicious group, Sybil grouping failure, omission, split fills and quote/settlement trust boundaries.
- TAP-VWAP-02: pin tapd/lnd revisions; regtest BTC->TAP and TAP->BTC, partial fills, failure, expiry, duplicate reporter, fee attribution and splice handling. Capture raw RFQ wire, HTLC/custom records, funding/asset proof, channel-state and fulfillment evidence. Publish redacted fixtures and hash manifests. A missing authenticated post-state is a blocking result, not a pass.
- TAP-VWAP-03: concrete receipt circuit with known-invalid tests, independent native verification, Nova fold and wrapper profiling. Show nullifier membership/absence and quote limits across epochs.
- TAP-VWAP-04: fixed-seed attack sweep, censorship, capital recycling, depeg, group availability, shared price feeds; compare raw/median/capped estimators under equal admission.
- TAP-VWAP-05: NEAR test verifier and SPV/finality adapter in isolation; cross-domain replay/reorg negatives; no consensus admission.
- TAP-VWAP-06: reviewed activation spec for TradeLayer and FROST DLC price attestations. BitVM3 is separate future enforcement. No real funds until independently reviewed activation.

TAP-VWAP-01A only exercises normalized, synthetic records; it does not authenticate any receipt.
